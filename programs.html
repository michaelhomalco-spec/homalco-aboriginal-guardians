Marine & Underwater Drone Surveys

Deploying remote-operated underwater vehicles (ROVs) to monitor sea sponge reefs, assess water quality, and protect fragile marine ecosystems across Bute Inlet.

Salmon & Fisheries Stewardship

Conducting vital fieldwork to track salmon returns, monitor spawning habitats, and safeguard aquatic life within traditional Homalco waters.

Cultural Heritage Protection

Documenting, mapping, and protecting ancient village sites, culturally modified trees (CMTs), and sacred areas for future generations.
