Active Project

Bute Inlet Sea Sponge Mapping

Utilizing high-definition underwater drone technology to map, study, and monitor deep-water glass sponge reefs in Bute Inlet to prevent anchor damage and bottom trawling impacts.

Salmon Escapement & Spawning Stream Surveys

Tracking annual salmon runs through habitat sampling, fish counting, and water flow monitoring across key watershed areas.

Territory Cultural Heritage Protection

Documenting and cataloging culturally modified trees (CMTs) and historical village sites across our coastal lands to preserve traditional heritage for generations to come.
